# TOAST — Teaching, Organization, Academics & Student Technology

## The Modern University Academic Operating System

> **TOAST is a purpose-built, high-concurrency Academic Operating System engineered from first principles for universities.**
> Built on a 100% modern distributed server stack with a Daylight-First (Light-Themed) Institutional UI, TOAST unifies teaching, learning, student productivity, institutional governance, document intelligence, and gamification into a single platform capable of serving **5,000 to 25,000+ concurrent users**.

---

# 1. Vision

Universities today force students, professors, and administrators to juggle a fragmented patchwork of tools:

* Legacy course portals for syllabi and file dumps
* Separate portals for attendance and timetables
* External spreadsheets for project viva and lab slot bookings
* Separate tools for assignments, coding labs, and timed quizzes
* External utilities to compress PDFs and DOCX files under 2 MB upload limits
* Disconnected task apps for deadline tracking
* Separate tools for notes, AI study assistance, and revision
* Disconnected scripts for degree credit tracking and accreditation (OBE / CO-PO) reports

**TOAST unifies the entire university lifecycle into one cohesive operating system.**

# 3. University User Roles & Experiences

## 3.1 Student Experience
The student opens TOAST to an immediate, zero-clutter **Academic Command Center**:
* **Top KPI Strip**: Overall Attendance % (with exact classes needed to stay $\ge 75\%$), Active Deadlines this week, Current SGPA/CGPA, and Academic XP/Streak.
* **Today's Live Schedule**: Chronological lecture & lab timeline with room numbers, instructor names, and a 1-click **"Check-In Attendance"** button when a session is active.
* **Smart Deadline Queue**: Prioritized pending assignments and quizzes with personal progress sliders (`0% → 100%`), milestone checklists, and direct submission modals.
* **Built-In Document Studio & Compressor**: Preview PDFs/DOCX in-browser, compress oversized submissions to `< 2 MB` in one click without leaving the assignment page, and pin files for offline reading.
* **Viva & Office-Hours Booking**: Book 15-minute project viva or lab evaluation slots directly from the course page.
* **AI Study Copilot & Global Search (`⌘K`)**: Ask questions grounded in the professor's uploaded lecture notes (*"Explain LR(1) parsing from Module 3 slides"*).
* **Alien Run**: Optional gamified world where academic XP unlocks levels, biome challenges, and rewards.

## 3.2 Faculty (Professor / Course Coordinator / TA) Experience
* **Today's Teaching & Live Attendance**: Launch a rotating 10-second QR code / 6-digit PIN on the classroom projector; watch real-time student check-ins populate via WebSockets.
* **Course & Syllabus Studio**: Drag-and-drop modules, upload lecture PDFs/videos, tag learning outcomes (`CO1–CO6`), and schedule announcements.
* **Split-Screen Speed Grader**: View student PDF/DOCX/Code submissions on the left, click rubric criteria on the right, leave inline feedback, and publish grades in bulk.
* **Assessment & Question Bank Studio**: Build timed quizzes with randomized question pools, LaTeX support, campus lab IP locks, and focus-loss telemetry.
* **Viva / Lab Slot Publisher**: Create time blocks for project evaluations; TOAST handles team slot booking and waitlists automatically.
* **At-Risk Student Watchlist**: Automatically highlights students in the section whose attendance is below 75% or who have missed 2+ consecutive deadlines.

## 3.3 Head of Department (HoD) & Dean Experience
* **Department Command Dashboard**: Real-time attendance averages, course completion progress, and grade distributions across all batches and semesters.
* **OBE & Accreditation Engine**: Automated Course Outcome (CO) to Program Outcome (PO) attainment calculations ready for accreditation audits.
* **Curriculum & Degree Program Manager**: Define 8-semester degree structures, credit requirements, prerequisite chains, and elective buckets.
* **Scheduled Executive Reports**: Receive automated Monday 8:00 AM PDF/XLSX digests of department attendance and academic health.

## 3.4 University Administrator / IT Directorate Experience
* **Multi-Campus & Organization Setup**: Manage campuses, schools, departments, academic years, semesters, and holiday calendars.
* **Bulk SIS & User Provisioning**: Import thousands of students, roll numbers, cohorts, and course enrollments via CSV or SIS API sync.
* **Dynamic Automation Rules Studio**: Configure institutional *"When [Trigger] + If [Condition] → Then [Action]"* workflows without writing code.
* **Server Health & Queue Monitor**: Inspect live PostgreSQL pool saturation, Redis memory, MinIO storage usage, and Document Compressor / AI worker queues.

# 4. Institutional Light-Themed UI Design System ("Daylight Academic")

University software is used for 6 to 10 hours a day in brightly lit lecture halls, computer laboratories, libraries, and faculty offices. TOAST is engineered **Light-Theme First** for maximum daytime readability, zero eye strain, and WCAG 2.1 AA+ contrast compliance (with an instant user-selectable Dark Mode toggle).

# 5. High-Concurrency Multi-User Server Architecture (5,000–25,000+ Users)

University platforms face **extreme synchronized concurrency bursts**:
1. **08:55–09:05 AM**: Thousands of students open dashboards and check into live attendance simultaneously.
2. **10:00:00 AM Exam Start**: 1,500+ students start a timed mid-semester quiz at the exact same second.
3. **11:55–11:59 PM Deadline Stampede**: Hundreds of students compress and upload multi-megabyte PDFs/DOCX files simultaneously.

### 5.1 Distributed Server Stack

```text
                       Users (5,000 - 25,000+ Concurrent)
                                       │
                        Nginx / Envoy Load Balancer + WAF
                                       │
         ┌─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                             ▼                             ▼
  Next.js 15 Cluster          TOAST Core API Cluster       WebSocket / SSE Cluster
 (Stateless SSR/Edge)        (FastAPI / Node.js Nodes)    (Live Attendance & Exams)
         │                             │                             │
         └─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┘
                                       │
         ┌─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                             ▼                             ▼
  Redis 7 Cluster                 PgBouncer Pooler           MinIO / S3 Storage
 • Sessions & JWT Revoke         (10k Conns → 200 PG)       • Presigned Uploads
 • Live Exam Answer Buffer             │                    • Course PDFs & Videos
 • SWR Dashboard Cache        ┌────────┴────────┐           • Compressed Vault
 • BullMQ / Celery Queues     ▼                 ▼
                       PostgreSQL 16     PostgreSQL 16
                          Primary         Read Replicas
                        (ACID Writes)   (Dashboards/Search)
                              │
                              ▼
               Isolated Background Worker Cluster
        ┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
        ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
  PDF/DOCX       Dynamic Rules   Exam Grader &   Privacy-First
 Compressor      & Notification  OBE Report      AI Course RAG
  Workers           Workers        Workers         Workers
```

### 5.2 Key Server Engineering Principles
* **Zero-Proxy Presigned File Uploads**: File bytes never pass through Core API memory. Clients upload directly to MinIO/S3 via presigned URLs and trigger async worker jobs.
* **Lock-In Submission Timestamps**: When a student submits a file for compression at `11:58:45 PM`, the submission timestamp is recorded immediately upon upload so background compression queue time never triggers a late penalty.
* **Redis-Buffered Live Exams**: Quiz answers are saved to Redis hashes in `< 5 ms` and batch-flushed to PostgreSQL asynchronously, eliminating database lock contention during mass exams.
* **PgBouncer Connection Multiplexing**: Pools thousands of stateless API and worker connections down to a tight set of physical PostgreSQL connections, with read-heavy dashboard and analytics queries routed to Read Replicas.

# 6. Phased Execution Roadmap

* **Phase 0 — University Server Foundation**: Monorepo setup, Docker Compose cluster (`PostgreSQL 16 + PgBouncer + Redis 7 + MinIO + Nginx`), Database migrations, RBAC & University SSO authentication, and Light-Themed UI Design System tokens.
* **Phase 1 — Core Academic OS & Dashboards**: University organizational hierarchy (Departments, Programs, Semesters, Sections), Student/Faculty/Admin Light-UI Dashboards, Course Syllabus Tree, Timetable, and Live QR/PIN Attendance.
* **Phase 2 — Productivity, Smart Deadlines & Document Studio**: Smart Deadline Priority Engine, Assignment & Rubric Workflows, Presigned S3 File Vault, and the asynchronous `< 2 MB` PDF/DOCX Compressor microservice.
* **Phase 3 — High-Concurrency Exams, Viva Booking & Gradebook**: Question Bank, Redis-buffered Timed Quiz Engine, Viva/Office-Hours Appointment Booking, and Credit-Weighted SGPA/CGPA Gradebook.
* **Phase 4 — Institutional Automation, Certificates & Analytics**: Dynamic *"If-This-Then-That"* Rule Engine, Verifiable QR Certificates, Predictive At-Risk Student Detection, OBE CO-PO Attainment, and Custom Report Builder.
* **Phase 5 — Privacy-First AI Course Intelligence**: Course-scoped RAG ingestion pipeline (`pgvector`), AI Study Copilot, PDF Summarizer, Faculty Quiz/Rubric Drafter, and `⌘K` Global Semantic Search.
* **Phase 6 — Gamification & Alien Run**: Event-driven Academic XP Ledger, Streaks, Badges, Section Leaderboards, and the interactive Alien Run progression world.
